Written offer of corresponding source (GPLv2 §3(b))

For at least three years from each image publish date, yet-another-solutions
will provide complete corresponding source for GPL components conveyed in
stripped-keycloak and stripped-keycloak-operator images, at no more than the
cost of physically performing source distribution.

Request it via GitHub Issues on:
  https://github.com/yet-another-solutions/stripped-keycloak

Or obtain the same source from the upstream locations pinned by the image:

  Alpine 3.24.1 aports:
    https://gitlab.alpinelinux.org/alpine/aports

  BusyBox (GPL-2.0-only), as packaged by Alpine:
    https://busybox.net/
    https://pkgs.alpinelinux.org/package/v3.24/main/x86_64/busybox

  Eclipse Temurin JDK used as the jlink input (versions in the Containerfile):
    https://github.com/adoptium/temurin-build
    https://github.com/adoptium/jdk21u
    https://github.com/adoptium/jdk25u
    https://adoptium.net/

  MySQL Connector/J as shipped in the official Keycloak 26.7.2 dist:
    https://github.com/mysql/mysql-connector-j

This offer does not apply to Apache-2.0 Keycloak or operator code, which is
not GPL.
